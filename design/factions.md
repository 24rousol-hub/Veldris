# The villain teams: Team Commons and the Drowned Crown

Status: the points marked **(author, 2026-10-04)** come from the author. Everything else is **PROPOSED** (CLAUDE.md rule 9). Pitch and research that led here: the 2026-10-04 chat (ideas 1 'the cover story' and 3 'the drowned crown', merged by the author).

## The short version (author, 2026-10-04)

Two groups, one **reluctant partnership** held together by hating the rich. The Goldsworths are an annoyance; these two are the real danger. The player cares about Veldris but feels this should not be their problem: a nuisance, but a necessary one ([game-bible.md](game-bible.md), pillar 1).

The growing anti-rich discourse explains why **only the Goldsworths have stayed in Veldris**, and not even the whole family: the other wealthy families have left.

## The Drowned Crown (author, 2026-10-04)

- The leader is **believed to be the last descendant of the kings and queens who ruled the waters from Aldermere before time was recorded**. 'Believed' leaves room for a twist (PROPOSED: the claim may be false, or true and irrelevant).
- **Maybe** that age is the same timeline as the mainline Legends game (Legends: Arceus). Open, see question 1.
- They are the **story climax: right after the last badge (9) and before the League**, because **the Elite Four and Cynthia have left to try to calm the region**.
- **The Goldsworths are not to blame** for whatever happened to the old crown: the family only rose to fame with the grandfather (Gatsby). But the Crown sees them as **the biggest obstacle**: huge influence inside Veldris, surely ties to powers outside it, and the potential to bring in outside help.
- The Goldsworth headquarters is **protected by the Elite Four and Cynthia because it is close to them** (the skyscraper is in Gildhaven, whose south gate is the Pinnacle Gate, see [maps/detail/gildhaven.md](maps/detail/gildhaven.md)). PROPOSED consequence: when the Elite Four and Cynthia leave to calm the region, the tower is left unguarded, and that is when the Crown strikes.

## Team Commons and the alliance (author, 2026-10-04)

- The player runs into the Commons **more personally**, and they **typically block land routes**.
- **The alliance breaks.** The player **temporarily teams up with the Commons, somewhere between badges 5 and 7**.
- The player then **battles the Commons leader alongside Troglodyte**. After losing, the leader **changes approach**, realising the Goldsworths are not actually evil, and that a lot of the problems blamed on them come from the Crown and from the Commons' own ignorance.
- PROPOSED order: the alliance breaks over the Crown's first casualties, the Commons and the player work together against the Crown, and the Commons leader's fight with the player and Troglodyte comes after (the leader still blames the Goldsworths and goes for the family directly). The author has not fixed the order of the team-up and the fight.

## How it fits what is already decided

- **Troglodyte, Arc A (contemptuous, then humbled):** fighting beside the player against the Commons leader is a natural humbling point. Which story points change him is still open (game-bible decision 13).
- **Gatsby:** the family's fame starts with him, which is why the Crown's grudge does not really fit the Goldsworths. He can be the one who knows the family's real history (PROPOSED).
- **Goldsworth houses:** 'not even the whole family' stayed, so some city houses can be **empty or boarded up** (PROPOSED; decide per city).

PARTNER_STEVEN`). Troglodyte needs a partner entry. Checked in this tree, not tested.
